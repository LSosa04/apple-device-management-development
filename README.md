# Apple Device Management Deployment

## Project Overview
Independently implemented an Apple device management
solution for a construction company, supporting
approximately 50+ company-owned iPads.

## Technologies Used
- Apple Business Manager (ABM)
- Apple Configurator
- Mosyle Mobile Device Management (MDM)
- Apple iPadOS

## My Responsibilities
- Created and configured Apple Business Manager.
- Implemented Mosyle MDM for company devices.
- Used Apple Configurator to enroll company iPads.
- Integrated device enrollment between ABM and Mosyle.
- Configured and prepared iPads for field personnel.
- Provided ongoing troubleshooting and device support.

## Device Enrollment Workflow
1. Prepare the iPad using Apple Configurator.
2. Add the device to Apple Business Manager.
3. Assign the device to Mosyle MDM.
4. Complete device enrollment and configuration.
5. Deploy the iPad to the assigned employee.

## Project Scale
Approximately 50+ company-managed iPads.
